Hello, I am Billy. I am a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Virginia and a member of the Automous Mobile Robots (AMR) Lab. My research is just starting but I am focusing on increasing safety and robustness of multi-robot systems. I leverage various techniques ranging from low level filters to high level understanding and planning to achieve desired and explainable behavior throughout the entire control stack.

Previously I was a Staff Scientist at Applied Research Associates. My work focused on maximizing intelligence and information from images. I emphasized designing around increased the flexibility of our systems and algorithms through deployment on different platforms from SBC's to servers.

Prior to joining ARA, I completed my master's and bachelor's degrees from the University of Virginia in Mechanical Engineering (2023 and 2021). As a graduate student my research focuses were 3D mapping and mobile manipulation with an emphasis on reducing operator setup and difficulty.

Outside of school and work, I enjoy running, biking, climbing, soccer, and anything that gets me outside into nature. I love watching F1 on the weekends and can not get enough of the amazing engineering that goes into their race cars. The rest of my weekend is spent maintaining, and hopefully soon riding, my 1970 Honda CB450.

Research

I have worked on a range of projects related to robotics and my expertise is in applied robotics based on a theoretical background. The aim of my research and work is to increase the usability of robotic / technical systems by reducing the entry barrier for non-experts seeking to leverage emerging technologies.

I enjoy the hands on interactions for both myself and robotic systems. There is nothing better than getting a robot to interact with the real world through seeing and moving just like we do. From mechantronics to robot design to computer vision to motion planning, through my experiences with different organizations and projects I have worked and studied many aspects of robotics to develop a wide variety of skills.
